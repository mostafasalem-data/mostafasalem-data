Follow-up KPI Dashboard

Power BI · DAX

A monthly dashboard where each KPI card is checked against its target and flagged when off-goal.

  • Filters by exchange and month, with drill-down to sub-exchanges.
  • Covers faults per 1000 customers, 24-hour repair rate, repeat faults and reassignment.
  • Ranks the sub-exchanges and cabinets with the biggest impact.

Monthly KPI Trend Report, Jan–Aug 2026

Power BI · KPI Analysis

A trend report that tracks every branch against its target month by month, so managers see who is drifting before the gap grows.

  • Covers all 6 exchanges across January to August 2026.
  • Six KPIs per branch: faults per 1000 customers, 24-hour fault clearance (data and voice), repeat-fault rate (data and voice), and technical provisioning.
  • Each chart carries the target line, so over- and under-performance is clear at a glance.
  • Feeds the monthly follow-up reports used for decision support.

Student Enrollment Dashboard

Excel · Pivot Tables · Slicers

An interactive Excel dashboard built on 94 student enrollment records across 6 courses.

  • Standardized mixed date formats into one clean date column and split course codes from course names.
  • Pivot tables for enrollments by course and by month, plus a Top 5 ranking.
  • Report sheet with slicers, a monthly trend line, a bar chart and a course-share doughnut.
  • KPI cards for course count, student count and average final score, with Course and Completion Status slicers (70 pass, 24 fail).
